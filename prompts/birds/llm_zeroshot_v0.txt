Classify the advertisement using only the supplied advert text.

Label 1: relevant - The advert offers live birds for sale, exchange or free transfer, is not a wanted/request advert, and does not offer excluded poultry taxa.

Label 2: not relevant - The advert does not offer live birds, requests birds rather than offering them, or offers excluded poultry taxa.

Excluded poultry taxa are all birds in Anseriformes (waterfowl) or Galliformes (gamebirds), and domestic pigeons. Apply these exclusions regardless of whether the birds are described as wild or domestic. Eggs, bird products and accessories alone are not live birds.

Return only relevant or not relevant.
