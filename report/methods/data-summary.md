Datasets were collected from two sources:

1. [Barbosa et al.’s LTS (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21211): Authors scraped marketplace advertisements pertaining to three distinct monitoring policies: shark-derived products, leather, and more general animal products, then manually labelled a validation subset of approximately 500 adverts for each. Only the Animal Products and Shark Products subsets were used in the present study; the Leather Products subset was excluded due to unresolved ambiguity between its annotation criteria and stated screening policy, which prevented reliable interpretation of model performance (see the [Limitations](#limitations) section for more detail).
2. [Stringham et al. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0254007): Authors manually labelled 16,509 advertisements sampled from an Australian bird-trading classifieds website, using titles, descriptions and images.

Further details on each dataset can be found in the [Dataset details](#selected-dataset) section.
