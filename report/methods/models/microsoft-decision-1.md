**Notes:**

Microsoft Decision-1 supplies a separate purpose-built decision model through OpenRouter, returning labelled choice probabilities. Its low listed inference price motivates inclusion as another hosted screening option. The configured endpoint is alpha and the provider describes continually updated weights, limiting exact reproducibility.
