- **Source:** [Barbosa et al. (2025)](https://github.com/VIDA-NYU/LTS/tree/main/data_use_cases)
- **Collection method:** Scoped crawl of eBay.com seeded with names of sharks, rays and chimaeras paired with associated body parts.
- **Labelling method:** Original expert-provided manual labels for 499 samples.
- **Processing:** N/A
- **Features:** Advert titles and descriptions.
- **Labels/policy:** The screening policy captures products derived from three animal groups: sharks, rays and chimaeras. It explicitly excludes fossils, replicas, unrelated products and single teeth.
- **Caveats:** Local inspection identifies apparent policy-label discrepancies, including a positive-labelled fossil advert. These remain unchanged.
