This SD describes policy variation, not confidence intervals or cross-validation-fold variation; sample sizes are not pooled or used as weights. Their balanced accuracy is derived from rounded fold-average recall and specificity in [Stringham et al. (2021), Appendix S2](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0254007.s002).
