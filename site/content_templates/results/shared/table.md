**Grouping:** Where available, check Input and/or Prompt to average across those conditions while keeping models and unchecked conditions separate. Each declared measured configuration has equal weight. Cells show the mean (± population SD); percentage SDs represent percentage-point variation, with no unit suffix printed inside the parentheses. This describes configuration variation, not uncertainty or a preferred operating configuration. Grouped TPR replaces the individual Wilson intervals; interval bounds are never averaged. Counts, including missed adverts and failures, may have fractional averages. Coverage and failures are summarized separately. Every member must supply a metric or that cell is N/A; any incomplete configuration withholds all performance summaries, while finished-with-failures results remain included. Hover over a grouped cell for availability details. Published rows remain individual reported values. Uncheck both controls to restore the original rows and intervals. Only available declared configurations are represented; absent conditions are not invented. Grouping is independent of the ROC filters, and each dataset retains its controls while switching tabs.
