# Figure 3. Zero-shot results across datasets

**Figure 3:** For individual measured models, white circles show the arithmetic mean across Shark Products (title + desc), Animal Products (title only), and Live Birds (title + desc), with each dataset weighted equally. Whiskers show ±1 population standard deviation across datasets, not confidence intervals or repeated-run uncertainty. Coloured dots show the individual datasets with fixed vertical offsets. All coloured dots and crosses, including legend symbols, use 85% opacity; white means use 100%. Higher balanced accuracy is better; lower values are better in the other panels. Measured model rows include only zero-shot configurations, independently of the ROC filters. Individual-model means and SDs require all three dataset values; N/A marks unavailable summaries; tooltips and the values table retain dataset coverage, and ambiguous dataset versions are not selected automatically. Chat has no recorded native-score FPR @ 95% TPR metric. API charges and summed saved request-seconds are shown per 1,000 adverts. Local d3 has zero external API charge; hardware and operating costs remain unknown. Summed request time is not elapsed batch time under concurrency. This descriptive summary does not establish significance or a preferred deployment model.

**Decision models (*μ*):** The white diamond is the equally weighted mean of all nine values from d3, Microsoft-Decision-1 and GPT-6 Luna Decisions across the three datasets. Its whiskers show ±1 population SD across those nine model–dataset values, so they describe variation across both models and datasets, rather than SD of the three dataset averages. Each coloured point averages the three decision models within that dataset. This row uses exactly the same zero-shot conditions as the individual rows: Shark Products title + desc, Animal Products title only, and Live Birds title + desc. Every coloured point requires all three model values; the white mean/SD requires all nine. Coverage, contributing models and values are available in tooltips and Summary values and availability. This is a descriptive average individual model, not an ensemble or pooled-prediction performance; resource values are average individual-model charges/times, not totals for running all three. Resource SD whiskers may extend below zero descriptively; this does not imply negative resource use.

The Supervised baselines row shows only the best available published baseline by estimated balanced accuracy for each dataset: ACTL text for Shark Products and LTS text for Animal Products. These are individual reference points, with no pooled mean or SD. Their balanced accuracy is estimated from published TPR and reconstructed FPR, conditional on positive-class metric interpretation and compatible dataset prevalence; it is not a directly reported score. The selected method therefore differs between datasets. Live Birds has no matching published composite-policy baseline, and comparable FPR @ 95% TPR, charge and request-time values are unavailable for this row.

A thin green **×** labelled **Live Birds (split policy)** shows the equally weighted mean balanced accuracy across three separate policy tasks (approximately **92.40%**), with capped whiskers showing ±1 population SD across those policy means (approximately **4.24 percentage points**). The underlying best-per-policy references are domestic poultry (Naive Bayes, approximately 98.05%), junk (Naive Bayes, 91.30%), and wanted adverts (logistic regression, 87.85%). Each policy value is derived as `100 × (mean recall + mean specificity) / 2` from rounded 10-fold cross-validation averages in [Stringham et al. (2021), Appendix S2](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0254007.s002). The eligible samples differ by policy (16,475, 16,446 and 13,751 adverts). This is descriptive variation across policy tasks, not a confidence interval, variation across CV folds or combined-policy bird performance. Policies have equal weight; sample sizes are not pooled or used as weights. Individual policy values, methods, samples, source and derivation remain in tooltips and Summary values and availability. This reference never enters measured-model summaries, including Decision Models, and supplies no target-recall or resource estimates. It is omitted with an explanation unless all three valid references and one unambiguous selected Live Birds dataset are available.

The grey band distinguishes the Supervised baselines row from measured model summaries.
